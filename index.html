Professional Summary

Geomatics professional (Engineer in Geomensura), university lecturer and researcher with more than 15 years of experience in geographic information, remote sensing, complex and dynamic systems, sustainability and territorial development. Completed the full coursework of a Doctorate in Sustainability. Professional with experience in various areas, including:

  • Leadership: Head of a university study program (Geomatic Engineering), representing the institution in various public and private forums.
  • Project Management: Expertise in the formulation, management, and evaluation of internationalization, research, innovation, outreach, extension, and teaching projects.
  • Strategy Development: Designed and implemented the OCRE Scientific School Observatory and created a network cooperation strategy for community engagement with local stakeholders.
  • Institutional Relations: Established agreements with municipalities, educational institutions, and companies; coordinated international relations of the Erasmus+ INCHIPE project.
  • Scientific Research: Focused on systems, complex systems, dynamic systems, and the study of urban metabolism theory for sustainability in cities.
  • Data Analysis: Skilled in machine learning, geospatial statistics, descriptive and inferential analysis, and time series analysis.
  • Publications: Authored articles and book chapters, contributed to international journals, and evaluated master's theses for prestigious institutions.
  • Conferences: Speaker at national and international conferences on topics such as macrocompetence learning, thematic quality evaluation, geographic information quality standards, forest fire causality indexes and IoT for geomatics.
  • Geomatic Engineering: Extensive experience in surveying, remote sensing, geodesy, cartography, GIS, and photogrammetry.
  • Geographic Information Quality: Specialist in evaluation of the quality of geographic information; member of the national mirror committee for ISO/TC211 (Geographic information / Geomatics).
  • Programming: Proficient in Python, R, JavaScript, and GIS tools.
  • Technical Assistance: Provided expertise in topographic and geodetic instrumentation, including stormwater drainage measurement post-earthquake.
  • Emerging Technologies: Technical diploma in Internet of Things and MIT Professional Education courses in sensory intelligence, digital platforms and product design strategy.
  • Software Proficiency: Advanced use of AutoCAD, office tools, and spatial analysis software.
  • Higher Education Teaching: Over 15 years of experience teaching geomatics, research methodology, GIS, remote sensing, and more at the university level.
  • Curriculum Development: Led the curricular redesign of the geomatic engineering program.
  • Mentorship: Sponsored and examined numerous professional habilitation and graduation projects in diverse topics related to geomatics.
  • Training: Conducted workshops on topics like blended and hybrid learning, entrepreneurship, and sustainability.
  • Public Market Tenders: Experienced in searching for and securing public market tenders, with a focus on geospatial and geomatic projects.
  • Technical Assistance: Provided technical support to public entities, including post-disaster assessments for municipalities.
  • Public Sector Experience: Engaged in international cooperation projects, enhancing research and internationalization in collaboration with public universities.
  • Communication: Delivered talks and workshops to schools, communities and professional audiences, translating technical topics into clear, actionable messages.
  • Entrepreneurship and Innovation: Trained as a trainer in entrepreneurship and innovation with a territorial focus; studied circular economy and product/platform strategy at MIT Professional Education.
  • Sustainability: Completed the full Doctorate in Sustainability coursework: global environmental problems, environmental economics and policy, ecological and resource modelling, environmental planning and assessment, environmental management, sustainable tourism and human settlements, climate change, disaster risk management and ecosystem rehabilitation.
  • Territorial Development: Applies geospatial analysis, simulation and sustainability frameworks to territorial planning, risk and urban sustainability problems.

Research and Publications

Research line: Psicosfera–Tecnósfera

Interdisciplinary line on how human processes, social relations, institutions, technologies and territorial systems interact. It combines territorial analysis, systems thinking, critical realism and computational tools (semantic and network analysis) to generate hypotheses, keeping empirical validation as a separate stage.

  • Results: 80 Chilean cities, 4 Resilience–Transformability (R–T) archetypes, 2005–2023; cases Copiapó, Talcahuano, Valdivia and Angol.
  • Pilot case: San Carlos de Purén (Biobío), exploratory methodological environment.
  • Open to interdisciplinary collaboration; citable in APA 7th edition format.
